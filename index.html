Privacy Policy — Words for Life

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Words for Life ("the App") is a children's vocabulary practice app for families and classrooms. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

Who this is for

The App is designed for children (roughly ages 4–12) with parent or teacher involvement. We take children's privacy seriously.

Summary

  • Reading practice works offline. iCloud Sync uses the Apple Account already signed in on your device.
  • The App stores reading data on your device. When iCloud Sync is on, supported data also syncs through your Apple Account.
  • We do not collect personal information on our servers.
  • We do not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell personal information.

Information stored on your device

Depending on how you use the App, it may store locally on the device:

  • Reading level progress and star progress
  • Words flagged for practice and related practice memory
  • Session resume checkpoints
  • Settings you choose (for example sound, high contrast, reduce motion, text size)
  • Per-reader profiles (name, school grade, picture choice, comfort preferences)
  • Practice stickers / related local rewards state
  • Weekly Sight Words lists (words, week status, and related progress for that week)
  • Optional photos of a teacher word sheet you choose to capture or pick for Weekly Sight Words (kept in the app's sandbox)

This data is stored using on-device storage (such as Apple's UserDefaults / app sandbox). It is not uploaded to us.

iCloud Sync

In app versions that support iCloud Sync, it is on by default when iCloud is available. The App uses the private iCloud storage associated with the Apple Account signed in on the device. Reader profiles, supported reader settings, reading progress, flagged words, review history, stars, stickers, weekly word lists, and saved teacher-sheet photos can sync to devices signed in to that same Apple Account. The App keeps the exact card where a reader stopped on the device. The App does not save microphone recordings.

In the App's Settings, Pause on This Device stops syncing on that device and leaves the iCloud copy in place. Delete iCloud Copy removes the synced reader data from that Apple Account and pauses sync on the device where you make the choice. Copies already on devices remain available.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect or receive:

  • Apple Account email addresses or sign-in credentials
  • Precise location
  • Contacts, teacher-sheet photos, or audio recordings
  • Advertising IDs or tracking identifiers for ads
  • Analytics events sent to third parties

The App does not include third-party ads, in-app purchases, or third-party analytics SDKs.

Camera / Weekly Sight Words

Weekly Sight Words may use the device camera (or the photo library, if you pick an existing picture) so you can capture a teacher word sheet and help fill that week's list. When you use that feature:

  • The App asks for camera permission only when you choose to take a photo of a word sheet
  • On-device text recognition (Apple's Vision) can read words from that picture on the device
  • You review the suggested words before they are added to the week
  • The App saves the photo you choose to keep as a JPEG without location metadata. When iCloud Sync is on, the saved JPEG syncs with the week. It may contain names or other information visible on the sheet.
  • You can also type the word list instead of using the camera

The App does not use the camera for anything other than this optional word-sheet capture. Deleting a week or uninstalling the App removes that week's stored photo from the device.

Speech / "Hear It"

The App may use the device's built-in speech features (Apple's system text-to-speech) to speak words and short example sentences aloud. That processing happens through the system on the device according to Apple's platform behavior. The App does not operate its own cloud speech service.

Speech Recognition

When you enable the Listen feature in Speech Helper, Words for Life uses your device's microphone to check if your child said the word shown on the screen. This speech recognition:

  • Runs entirely on your device using Apple's on-device speech recognition
  • Only listens for the single word displayed, not continuous conversation
  • Does not save or store any audio recordings
  • Does not upload audio to any server. Apple's on-device recognition never sends audio to the cloud
  • Requires iOS 26 or later with the speech recognition model downloaded to the device

The Listen feature is off by default. When off, the app never accesses the microphone and never prompts for microphone permission. You can enable or disable it anytime in Settings, Speech Helper.

Sharing progress

Parents or teachers may choose to share a reading practice snapshot (for example via the system share sheet) after a parental gate. Sharing is initiated by the user and uses the device's share options (Messages, Mail, Files, etc.). We do not operate a cloud share service for that content.

Children's privacy (COPPA / Kids Category)

Because the App is intended for a child audience:

  • We do not require children to provide personal information to use the App.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or behavioral tracking.
  • Outbound sharing and outbound links from the App are protected by a parental gate where required for the Kids Category.

How to clear data

In the App's Settings, you can clear practice words and/or reset star progress (each action explains what it removes and asks for confirmation). Uninstalling the App also removes its on-device data. Deleting a reader profile removes that reader's local progress on the device. Deleting a Weekly Sight Words week removes that week's words, progress, and any stored teacher-sheet photo for that week.

App Store privacy labels

In App Store Connect, the App's privacy nutrition labels should reflect that the App does not collect data off the device for us to receive. Camera and photo-library access support optional Weekly Sight Words capture. The iCloud Sync section above describes what the App sends to Apple’s private iCloud storage.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update the "Last updated" date and the hosted copy of this page. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
